# My Radio Show

Since a couple of weeks I have this idea in my head to create some kind of radio show. Initially this is to be a gift for Hannah but the underlying message for myself is to curate and create something I am passionate about. It is a gift to others but also a form of creating that exites me and alligns with things I am rather passionate about. 

## Things to include
basically anything that can be audible. From short stories to music to poems to thoughts. Anything that can be orally shared or audibly perceived can be woven in this creation.

Examples;
- Songs.
- Poems.
- Short Stories.
- Personal Experiences.
- Journal Entries.
- Jokes.
- News.
- Introspective thoughts.
- Work-outs.
- Audio snippets from Speakers.
- Rants (?)
- Meditations.

**[RUNAWAY - HALF ALIVE] (3min)**

Afterwards I'd like to dissect some of the topics discussed in this song.
```
"I hold my life out in front of me, dreams of who I wanna be
I'm seeing every empty page
But I find that everything I am is everything I should be
Yeah, I don't need to run away"
```
The idea of having hope for the future can be both exciting and daunting. Daunting of the empty pages yet to fill (and how they need to be something worth writing about)
I see growing up as making choices which inevitably means sacrificing options. However, this song shows me an alternative where growing up doesn't mean letting go of certain dreams but showing appreciation for the current state of things. Not to be caught up in the future, only thinking ahead, but appreciating and trusting the process. It is a reminder of how real process doesn't come from having a goal or a vision but rather seeing the path you are on currently.

Question to ask the listener; I can't place the runaway part quite yet. I can feel a sense of running away from the present with the idea of "always needing to be better" (which is always orientated towards what is to come) but I wonder if I'm missing an interpretation. What do you think? And as a gentle nudge for some introspection: What are you running away from?.
